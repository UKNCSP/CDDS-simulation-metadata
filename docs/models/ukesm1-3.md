# UKESM1-3-LL
UKESM1-3 is based on UKESM1(the ESM model used in CMIP6). Like UKCM2-0-LL, it works at a resolution of approx. 130 km in the atmosphere and approx. 1° in the ocean. It is an extension of the CMIP6+ model, UKESM1.1 incorporating interactive ice sheet models for Greenland and Antarctica and closed CO2 and CH4 cycles. UKESM1.3 is expected to be unique in including the CH4 emission-driven capability.
