# UKESM1-3-LL
[UKESM1-3](https://ukesm.ac.uk/science/model-components/) is based on UKESM1(the ESM model used in CMIP6). Like [UKCM2-0-LL](https://egusphere.copernicus.org/preprints/2026/egusphere-2026-1676/), it works at a resolution of approx. 130 km in the atmosphere and approx. 1° in the ocean. It is an extension of the CMIP6+ model, UKESM1.1 incorporating interactive ice sheet models for Greenland and Antarctica and closed CO2 and CH4 cycles. [UKESM1-3](https://ukesm.ac.uk/science/model-components/) is expected to be unique in including the CH4 emission-driven capability.
