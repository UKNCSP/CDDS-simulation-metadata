# AerChemMIP
[AerChemMIP](https://www.wcrp-climate.org/modelling-wgcm-mip-catalogue/cmip6-endorsed-mips-article/1057-modelling-cmip6-aerchemip) (Aerosols and Chemistry Model Intercomparison Project) is a set of climate model experiments designed under CMIP6 to quantify the climate and air quality impacts of aerosols and chemically-reactive gases in climate models. It is hoped that these will help to understand the contribution of aerosols and chemistry to climate change.

For CMIP7, phase 2 of the Aerosol Chemistry Model Intercomparison Project ([AerChemMIP2](https://wcrp-cmip.org/mips/aerchemmip2/)) focuses on the quantification of the climate, atmospheric composition, and air quality responses to changes in emissions of aerosols, short lived climate forcers and chemically reactive gases. AerChemMIP2 aims to provide continuity of the results with CMIP7 models compared to CMIP6. New experiments in AerChemMIP2 aim to address some persistent challenges in Earth system modeling (e.g. the role of desert dust and biomass burning aerosols, as well as methane for atmospheric composition and climate).
