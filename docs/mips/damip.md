# DAMIP
[DAMIP](https://www.wcrp-cmip.org/mips/damip/) (Detection and Attribution Model Intercomparison Project) as of CMIP6 aimed to understand the individual contributions of various external factors to past and future changes in global and regional climate. This MIP aims to improve estimation of the contributions of anthropogenic and natural forcing changes to observed global warming, as well as observed global and regional climate changes. This will contribute to the estimation of how historical emissions have altered and are altering contemporary climate risk and to improve observationally-constrained projections of future climate change.
