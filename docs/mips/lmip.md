# LMIP
[LMIP](https://wcrp-cmip.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/2026-02-19_MIP-showcase_LMIP.pdf) (Land Model Intercomparison Project) runs land only simulations to evaluate land surface fluxes, snow cover and soil moisture representation. These aim to investigate ong-term energy, water, and carbon cycles driven by observation-constrained historical forcing and quantify how these processes contribute to biases and regional temperature/precipitation predictability.
