# HighResMIP
[HighResMIP](https://www.highresmip.org/) (High Resolution Model Intercomparison Project) aims to improve the understanding of model resolution/grid-spacing on climate simulation. [CMIP7 HighResMIP](https://highresmip.org/experiments/experiment_cmip7) has evolved their experiments from CMIP6 to include more idealised simulations to better link with CMIP, as well as shorter simulations to link with km-scale modelling efforts.


## Scientific Questions / Motivations
1. Can HighResMIP simulations help to better quantify and even reduce key structural uncertainties in future climate projections?
2. What resolution-dependent atmospheric or oceanic processes are missing from standard resolution models that might produce different future projections?
3. Can we combine infoormation from HighResMIP and CMIP7, together with other data sources (e.g. km-scale), to produce more robust plans for future adaptation and climate risk planning?
