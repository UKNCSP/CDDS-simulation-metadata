# RFMIP
[RFMIP](https://egusphere.copernicus.org/preprints/2025/egusphere-2025-4378/) (Radiative Forcing Model Intercomparison Project) aims to enable the systematic characterisation of effective radiative forcing and its components across climate models. This collection focusses fixed-sea surface temperature timeslice and transient experiments as well as those that address questions about the definition of radiative forcing, its sensitivity to evolving surface conditions and Earth’s energy budget. This serves as an expansion of core RFMIP experiments introduced in CMIP6 to allow for a consistent estimate for radiative forcing across model generations.
