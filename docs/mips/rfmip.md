# RFMIP
[RFMIP](https://egusphere.copernicus.org/preprints/2025/egusphere-2025-4378/) (Radiative Forcing Model Intercomparison Project) aims to enable the characterisation of effective radiative forcing and its components across climate models. This focusses on fixed-sea surface temperature timeslice and transient experiments. It also looks at experiments that address questions about the definition of radiative forcing and its sensitivity to evolving surface conditions and Earth’s energy budget. This serves as an expansion of core RFMIP experiments introduced in CMIP6 to allow for a consistent estimate for radiative forcing across model generations.
