# GeoMIP
[GeoMIP] (https://climate.envsci.rutgers.edu/geomip/) (Geoengineering Model Intercomparison Project)  as of CMIP6 presented of experiments designed to understand key uncertainties in extreme events, the use of geoengineering as part of a portfolio of responses to climate change, and cirrus cloud thinning as a method to allow more longwave radiation to escape to space ([WCRP, 2020](https://www.wcrp-climate.org/modelling-wgcm-mip-catalogue/cmip6-endorsed-mips-article/1054-modelling-cmip6-geomip)). For CMIP7, experiments have been organised into 3 tiers aimed to standardised SRM (Solar Radiation Modification) experiment protocol and enerating information useful for understanding potential climate and societal impacts of SRM ([EGUsphere, 2026](https://egusphere.copernicus.org/preprints/2026/egusphere-2026-2417/)).
