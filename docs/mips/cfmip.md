# CFMIP
[CFMIP](https://www.cfmip.org/) (Cloud Feedback Model Intercomparison Project) aims to improve the understanding and evaluation of clouds, cloud feedbacks, and their coupling with circulation, precipitation, and climate. CFMIP includes foundational experiments in the [CMIP AR7 Fast Track](https://www.wcrp-cmip.org/cmip-phases/cmip7/#cmip7_assessment_fast_track_cmip7_aft) to assess climate sensitivity and to understand patterns of surface warming.
