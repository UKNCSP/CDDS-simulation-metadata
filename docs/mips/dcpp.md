# DCPP
[DCPP](https://www.wcrp-esmo.org/projects-and-panels/dcpp) (Decadal Climate Prediction Project) focusses on annual, multi-annual and decadal predictions through predictability studies and retrospective forecasts ("hindcasts") using climate models and statistical approaches. These studies primarily focus on temperature and the prediction of variations in annual means over much of the globe across several years, conditional on the initialization of the forecasts. As part of the [CMIP AR7 Fast Track](https://www.wcrp-cmip.org/cmip-phases/cmip7/#cmip7_assessment_fast_track_cmip7_aft) DCPP is coordinating a 10-year long initialised prediction using CMIP6-era prediction systems and forcing.
