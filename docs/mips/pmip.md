# PMIP
[PMIP](https://pmip.lsce.ipsl.fr/) (Paleoclimate Modelling Intercomparison Project) coordinates model experiments for key past periods including when the climate was at equilibirum and simulations of past changes. These experiments are designed to study the impact of changes in boundary conditions, external forcings and feedbacks or new processes progressively added in climate models. There are also efforts to benchmark model results based on paleo-data by comparing simulations of past climates against paleo-observations.
