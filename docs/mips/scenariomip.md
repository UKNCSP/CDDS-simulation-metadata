# ScenarioMIP
[ScenarioMIP](https://wcrp-cmip.org/mips/scenariomip/) (Scenario Model Intercomparison Project) carries out earth system model simulations driven by alternative plausible futures of emissions and land use. These include a set of scenarios intended to be run in emission-driven mode ([GMD, 2026](https://gmd.copernicus.org/articles/19/2627/2026/)) exploring high levels of climate change (to explore high-end climate risks), medium levels of climate change (anchored to current policy), and low levels of climate change (aligned with current international agreements). This also includes plans for long-term extensions to study long-term impacts, climate change-related processes on long timescales and (ir)reversibility.
