# C4MIP
[C4MIP](https://www.c4mip.net/) aims to quantify carbon-climate feedbacks, carbon budgets, and assess what happens after reaching net-zero emissions ([WCRP, 2026](https://www.wcrp-cmip.org/cmip-2026-carbon-cycle-fire-and-earth-system-dynamics-key-insights-from-recent-research/)). Carbon-cycle feedbacks are potentially large and play a big contribution in determining the atmospheric composition in response to human CO2 emissions and in setting emissions targets to stabilize climate or avoid dangerous climate change.
